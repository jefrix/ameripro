PROCESS BOUNDARY

UF is not a blanket dissolved-contaminant removal claim. Salts, surfactants, dissolved organics and other feed-specific constituents can limit reuse even when suspended solids are reduced.

This train is not an MBR: no biological reactor, sewage-treatment performance or nutrient-removal capability is included. Source-specific alternatives require a separate design basis.

Calculation basis and model limits

Average feed = nominal GPM × availability / 100. Scheduled feed gallons = average feed × 60 × scheduled hours. Product = feed × net recovery fraction. Liquid residuals = feed − product. Internal recycle is excluded from the net feed / product boundary; chemical makeup, rainfall and evaporation are not modeled.

Equalization changes at collection inflow minus average feed. Product hold fills at average product rate with release inhibited. In the assumed-release scenario, uniform demand offsets product flow; if demand exceeds production, unmet demand is flagged rather than supplied from an empty tank. Residual storage receives the net liquid waste rate. Tanks are not emptied mid-period.

First intervention is the earliest positive or immediate equalization-full, equalization-empty, product-full or residual-full event. The displayed operating window is the smaller of this event time and scheduled hours. Treatment can require throttling or rescheduling after an intervention; full-schedule outputs must not be read as achieved production. The model uses averaged rates and does not capture batch backwash spikes, hourly inflow surges, solids mass or quality failures.
