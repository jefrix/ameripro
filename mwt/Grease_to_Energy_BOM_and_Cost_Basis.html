Batch contribution model

The interactive page uses a hypothetical $0.45/kg price for buyer-accepted oil-rich product, $100/batch collection cost not already covered by pumping charges, $120/batch processing allocation and $0.08/kg handling for aqueous residual plus wet cake. All are editable placeholders. No local market, buyer quote, tipping fee or disposal quote is represented.

The model books zero sales revenue while qualification is pending. If buyer acceptance is assumed, revenue equals modeled oil-rich product mass times entered price. Contribution subtracts acquisition / collection, processing and residual handling. The default scenario produces a negative contribution even with assumed sales.

Avoided disposal credit. Most GREX feed would come from AmeriPro's own grease-trap pumping, which customers already pay for. Trap waste processed at GREX no longer goes to an outside receiving facility, so the tipping fee AmeriPro pays today becomes a credit to each batch. The calculator has an input for this credit (default $0, since the fee is AmeriPro-specific) and reports the credit needed to break even. At the defaults, that is about $289.54 per 1,000 kg batch on hold, or $230.69 with assumed product sales.

Fixed overhead, capital recovery, financing, taxes, storage, product rejection / disposal, depreciation, residual classification changes, buyer testing and any downstream fuel conversion are excluded. Holding product can still incur additional costs not calculated. No “free disposal,” avoided pumping or environmental credit is booked automatically.

Required commercial evidence

Obtain source-separated feed analyses, actual collection and handling costs, representative separator / conditioning trials, prospective buyer specifications and written pricing, and receiving costs for each residual. Include storage / logistics and rejected batches. Verify all equipment and utility scopes before selecting a site or assigning a project budget.
