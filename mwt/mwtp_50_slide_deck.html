Why a Compact 35–50 GPM Unit Fits AmeriPro's Restaurant Base

02 • COMMERCIAL KITCHEN DEMAND PROFILE

What a Commercial Kitchen Actually Needs During an Advisory

A busy full-service restaurant uses 4,000–10,000 gallons/day (6–15 GPM average), with short 35–50 GPM peak bursts.

Fixture-by-Fixture Kitchen Demand 60–75 PSI Required
  • Dish Machine (12–18 GPM bursts): Needs ≥ 20 PSI dynamic flow (60 PSI line pressure) for sanitizing rinse cycles; main-break sediment clogs solenoid screens.
  • 3-Compartment & Prep Sinks (8–15 GPM): Continuous flow during food prep and pot washing.
  • Ice Machines, Soda & Espresso (2–5 GPM): Direct ingestion hazard during a Boil Water Advisory; needs 0.02 µm UF + carbon block polish + UV + Cl₂.
  • Restroom Flushometers (10–20 GPM bursts): Commercial tankless valves require ≥ 35–45 PSI at 15+ GPM just to close properly.
How MWTP-50 Buffers 50 GPM Peaks Steady CT + Surge Buffer
  • Steady 35 GPM Treatment Train: UF, UV, and the 600-gal baffled contact tank (TK-401) never exceed 35 GPM so 4-log virus contact time (T10 = 8.57 min) is never compromised.
  • Onboard 150-Gal Surge Clearwell (TK-601): Positioned over the tandem axles downstream of the contact tank.
  • Duplex 3 HP VFD Boosters (P-601A/B): Draw from the 150-gal clearwell to deliver up to 50 GPM peak bursts at 60–75 PSI whenever the dish pit and restrooms peak together.

03 • FLEXIBLE RAW WATER SUPPLY

Three Feed Modes — Including Zero-Hydrant Boil Water Advisory Mode

Every supply mode feeds the 550-gal collapsible break tank (TK-100) through a physical air gap and a 20 PSI pressure-sustaining valve (PSV-100).

MODE 1A • NO HYDRANT NEEDED PRIMARY BWA MODE

Pre-Installed Service-Line Tee

During a Boil Water Advisory where the street main still has pressure (40–70 PSI), the MWTP-50 draws 15–35 GPM from a 1.5" valved tee the restaurant's plumber installs on the service line between the meter and the building valves (set up at the site survey, with utility approval), through PSV-100 into TK-100. Two closed building valves keep raw and treated water apart — no hydrant permit needed.

MODE 1B • PERMITTED HYDRANT ≥ 20 PSI RESIDUAL

Municipal Fire Hydrant

Connects via utility hydrant meter and backflow assembly through 1.5" pressure-sustaining valve PSV-100 (set to hold the street main at ≥ 20 PSI residual) into the TK-100 air-gap fill nozzle.

MODE 1C • WATER MAIN OUTAGE 1 DROP / DAY

Single Potable Tanker Delivery

Whereas a 250 GPM hospital plant requires 15,000 gal/hr of continuous tanker shuttles during a zero-pressure outage, a restaurant using 4,000–6,000 gal/day can run all day on 1 or 2 potable tanker drops into TK-100.

Taste & Odor Bonus: Duplex #2 pre-filter housings (BF-101A/B) accept radial-flow activated carbon block cartridges to polish off pipe odors for coffee, tea, and fountain drinks. NSF-42 / NSF-61
04 • SAME REGULATORY RIGOR AS MWTP-250

6-Stage Treatment Train & Verified 4-Log Virus CT

Every pathogen credit is backed by online verification: daily UF pressure-decay test, UVDGM-validated UV, and measured contact time.

STAGE 1 & 2 • INTAKE & PRE-FILTER

550-Gal Break Tank, 3 HP Pump & Duplex #2 Bags

P-101 (3 HP VFD) pulls 40 GPM from TK-100 through a 1.5" 200 µm auto-strainer and duplex 25 µm bag/carbon housings, followed by inline PACl dosing.

STAGE 3 • 0.02 µm ULTRAFILTRATION

4 × 60" PVDF Modules + Daily DIT-201

2,200 sq.ft. membrane area (24.2 GFD flux at 35 GPM). 60-inch vertical modules stand 6.5 ft tall to fit under a 7.5-ft roof. Automated daily pressure-decay test verifies ≥ 4-log protozoa removal.

STAGE 4 • 100% REDUNDANT UV

2 × 40 GPM UV Reactors (1 Duty + 1 Standby)

UVDGM-validated ≥ 40 mJ/cm² dose with online UVT analyzer (AIT-401). Automatic duty-swap if lamp intensity drops.

STAGE 5 • 4-LOG VIRUS CHLORINE CT

600-Gal Baffled Ground Contact Tank (TK-401)

At 35 GPM, HDT = 17.1 min and T10 = 8.57 min (0.50 baffle factor). At 1.5 mg/L free Cl₂, CT = 12.85 mg·min/L — exceeding 4-log virus requirements even at 0.5 °C!

STAGE 5B • CHEMICAL SAFETY

Segregated Vented Cabinets & Gas Sensor

Separate bunded enclosures for 12.5% NaOCl vs. citric acid / bisulfite prevent accidental mixing, backed by AIT-901 Cl₂ gas detector, exhaust fan, and ANSI eyewash.

STAGE 6 • 50 GPM KITCHEN BOOSTER

150-Gal Clearwell + Duplex 3 HP VFD + 2" RPZ

P-401 transfers CT-verified water into the 150-gal onboard clearwell; duplex 3 HP boosters (P-601A/B) hold 60–75 PSI through an ASSE 1013 RPZ (RPZ-602).

POWER MODE A • BOIL WATER ADVISORY $0 Fuel / Silent

During a Boil Water Advisory, the restaurant still has grid power. Plugging into a pre-installed 240V 50A NEMA 14-50 / CS6365 receptacle runs the entire treatment plant silently next to outdoor dining patios with zero diesel exhaust.

POWER MODE B • STORM / GRID OUTAGE 47-Hr Fuel Tank

When grid power is down or no 50A plug is available, the sound-attenuated 20 kW Tier-4 Final diesel generator (62 dBA) on the gooseneck deck runs for 47 hours continuously on its 40-gallon tank (0.85 gal/hr).

AUTOMATIC QUALITY SHUTOFF (XV-602) Fail-Safe Closed

Potable discharge valve XV-602 locks shut automatically if laser turbidity (AIT-201) exceeds 0.15 NTU, UV dose drops below 40 mJ/cm², free chlorine drops below 0.8 mg/L, or calculated 4-log virus CT falls below 1.0× required.

HEALTH INSPECTOR COMPLIANCE LOGS 1-Click Export

The UL-508A PLC/HMI (CP-701) prints or exports a timestamped PDF compliance report (daily DIT pressure-decay pass, turbidity, UV dose, and chlorine CT) + certified lab Bac-T chain-of-custody for the county health inspector.

07 • RESTAURANT FIELD SOP & GREASE TRAP PROTECTION

Arrival to Pressurized Kitchen in ~2 Hours

Mapped in advance during regular AmeriPro F.O.G. and hood cleaning service visits.

0:00–0:30

1. Alley Park, Level & Plug In

Back 22-ft gooseneck into 2–3 parking spaces beside utility door, set 4 stabilizer jacks, plug into 240V 50A shore power (or start 20 kW gen), and unfold 550-gal & 600-gal ground tanks.

0:30–1:00

2. Isolate Street & Connect Supply

Close both building valves (double isolation) so treated water cannot backfeed the street. Connect the pre-installed service tee or hydrant via PSV-100 (≥ 20 PSI) to TK-100 air gap. Connect waste hose to downstream sanitary cleanout.

1:00–1:40

3. DIT Test, Prove CT & Sanitize Hose

Run 5-min automated UF pressure-decay test (DIT-201), turn over 600-gal contact tank until CT and Cl₂ are green, pull Bac-T sample, and sanitize the 1.5"/2" potable hose with the 50 mg/L kit.

1:40–2:00

4. Soft-Ramp Kitchen to 65 PSI

Fill kitchen lines at 30 PSI while bleeding air at the farthest 3-comp sink, then ramp duplex VFD boosters to 65 PSI. Verify ≥ 1.0 mg/L free Cl₂ at point of use.

SOP Rule 4 — F.O.G. Grease Trap Protection: Never discharge 35–60 GPM UF backwash water into a kitchen drain upstream of the grease trap! Always use the downstream sanitary sewer cleanout on the street side of the grease interceptor. Zero Grease Washout
