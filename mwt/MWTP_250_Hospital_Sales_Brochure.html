Building Pressurization — Not Just Buckets of Drinking Water

Tanker trucks fill ground storage but can't keep risers, fixtures, and sterile processing pressurized. The MWTP-250 feeds the building's domestic system directly through an RPZ backflow preventer.

1. Sterile Processing

Steady pressure and <0.05 NTU membrane-filtered water to the building system that feeds your sterilizers and washers (your existing in-house treatment stays in place).

2. Cooling Towers — Plan Separately

Tower makeup can use several hundred GPM on its own. We size domestic service first and work with your engineers on whether any tower makeup fits within the 250 GPM limit.

3. Patient Sanitation & Flushing

Pressurizes plumbing risers so toilets, hand sinks, showers, and labs keep working. Re-pressurization is coordinated with your water management (Legionella) program team.

4. Dietary, Kitchen & Ice

Gives your campus an alternate treated source under the state's emergency approval, so dietary and patient hydration can continue as the health authority allows.

MONITORED, CREDIT-BASED TREATMENT

6-Stage Treatment + Automated Fail-Safe Lockouts

Each barrier is monitored continuously, operated by a state-certified operator, and run under the state drinking water program's emergency-use approval:

  1. Break-Tank Supply That Protects the City Main: Hydrant water passes through the utility's meter and backflow device and a pressure-sustaining valve that holds the main at ≥ 20 PSI, then into an air-gap break tank. No pressure? We switch to tanker supply.
  2. 200 µm Auto-Strainer & Duplex 25 µm Pre-Filtration: Captures pipe rust, sand, and debris stirred up by main breaks.
  3. 0.02 Micron PVDF Ultrafiltration (UF): Physical membrane barrier for Giardia, Cryptosporidium, and bacteria (<0.05 NTU), with a daily pressure-decay integrity test to prove the membranes are intact.
  4. Validated UV (≥ 40 mJ/cm², 2 duty + 1 standby): A second, independent barrier for Giardia and Cryptosporidium.
  5. Chlorine With Measured Contact Time: A baffled contact tank gives the free chlorine time to inactivate viruses (4-log); the control system calculates contact time live and holds 1.5–2.0 mg/L residual.
  6. ASSE 1013 RPZ Backflow & Continuous Monitoring: Online turbidity, UV, and chlorine analyzers automatically close the building outlet if any reading goes out of range. Bacteriological samples go to a state-certified lab.
