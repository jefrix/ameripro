    Why Split the Power:

    Most restaurant calls are Boil Water Advisories, where the kitchen still has power. Taking the generator off the water trailer saves 1,160 lb, removes diesel from the alley, and lets one PWR-25 back up the whole fleet on the few outage jobs.
