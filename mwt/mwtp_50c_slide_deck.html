01 • WHY SPLIT THE POWER OFF

A Kitchen Without Power Is Closed Anyway

Restaurants need city power to cook. On almost every restaurant job the building already has power — so an onboard generator mostly rides along unused.

1. Lighter, Simpler Water Trailer

No diesel, no fuel tank, no generator compartment, no transfer switch. On-site weight drops from 9,820 lb to 8,660 lb — a 1,330 lb margin under the 9,990 lb rating.

2. A Generator We Control

Rental generators disappear right after hurricanes and ice storms. An owned PWR-25 is always available — and one generator trailer backs up two or three water trailers.

3. A Healthier Engine

A diesel that runs a few weeks a year clogs its exhaust filter and won't start when needed. Between emergencies the PWR-25 works other AmeriPro jobs or runs monthly against a load bank.

Both trailers stay under 10,000 lb, each behind a standard 1-ton pickup — any trained AmeriPro technician with a regular license and DOT medical card can tow either one. No Class A CDL

03 • REV C TREATMENT TRAIN

Six Stages, Nothing on the Ground

City water feeds the plant directly through a backflow preventer, and UV carries the virus kill — so the break tank and contact tank stay home on Boil Water Advisory jobs.

STAGE 1
Direct Feed

Service-line tee or hydrant → PSV-100 (holds ≥ 20 PSI) → 1.5" RPZ-100

STAGE 2

STAGE 3

STAGE 4 · NEW
Virus-Rated UV

186 mJ/cm² validated, duty + standby, live dose calculation

STAGE 5
Chlorine Residual

1.5–2.0 mg/L to protect the building's pipes — no contact tank

STAGE 6
Kitchen Booster

150-gal clearwell, duplex 3 HP VFD, 2" RPZ-602 → 60–75 PSI

Automatic lockouts: turbidity above 0.15 NTU, UV dose below 186 mJ/cm², chlorine outside 0.5–4.0 mg/L, or loss of power all close the building outlet XV-602.
Grease-trap rule: backwash and flush water always go to the sanitary cleanout downstream of the grease interceptor — never through it.

05 • WATER SUPPLY

Port A for Boil Water Jobs, Port B for Outages

The trailer never pulls the city main below 20 PSI, and two closed building valves always separate raw water from treated water.

PORT A · BOIL WATER ADVISORY (MOST JOBS)

Pressurized Direct Feed

  • 1.5" valved tee installed on the service line by the restaurant's plumber at the site survey, or a permitted hydrant with the utility's meter and backflow device.
  • PSV-100 holds the upstream line at 20 PSI or more.
  • RPZ-100 (ASSE 1013) replaces the air gap; a flow switch alarms if its relief port discharges.
  • The intake pump only boosts what the UF needs — less power than drafting from a tank.
PORT B · ZERO MAIN PRESSURE

Tanker or Folding Break Tank

  • The intake pump drafts straight from a potable tanker — the tanker is the break tank.
  • If the tanker can't stay, the 550-gal folding tank from the PWR-25 kit box is set up and filled by drops.
  • A restaurant uses 4,000–8,000 gal/day, so one 4,000-gal drop covers most of a service day.
  • Port A stays closed and capped.
