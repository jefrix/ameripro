Conduit, Cable & Segregation Rules, VFD Phase-Derating Spec & Commissioning Megger Test

Mandatory fabrication and field wiring instructions for trailer vibration, wet-location washdown, and EMI/RFI noise immunity.

1. Three-Raceway Separation Rule (UL-508A / IEEE 518)

Never pull VFD motor leads and 4–20 mA analyzer signals in the same conduit or wireway trough! Divide all trailer wiring into three physically separated raceways:

  • Raceway Group A (Power & VFD PWM): 1000V XLPE braided-shield VFD cable (Belden 2950x or Lapp ÖLFLEX VFD) in dedicated EMT / PVC-coated Liquidtight Flexible Metal Conduit (LFMC). Ground shield 360° at both VFD gland and motor peckerhead.
  • Raceway Group B (120VAC & 24VDC Solenoids): THWN-2 stranded copper (#14 min for power, #16 min for 24VDC solenoids) with flyback suppression diodes across every Namur solenoid coil.
  • Raceway Group C (4–20mA / Ethernet Instrumentation): Belden 8760 #18 AWG twisted shielded pair (TSP). Ground drain wire at the CP-701 analog ground bar ONLY (float and tape at field transmitter end to prevent ground loops).
2. Single-Phase Input VFD Sizing Rule (MWTP-50 Only)

On the MWTP-50-GN22, standard 230V 3-phase NEMA Premium Efficient washdown motors (P-101, P-401, P-601A/B) are driven from the 240V single-phase bus using VFDs as phase converters:

  • 50% Frame Derating: Feeding single-phase AC into a 3-phase VFD rectifier bridge increases DC bus ripple and diode current by √3. Therefore, all 3.0 HP motors (9.6A 3Φ) MUST use a 5.0 HP-rated 230V VFD frame (e.g., Allen-Bradley PowerFlex 525 25B-B017N104, rated 17A 3Φ / 9.6A 1Φ-in), and the 1.5 HP motor (P-401) uses a 3.0 HP VFD frame.
  • Mandatory 3% Input Line Reactor: Install a 3% impedance AC line reactor ahead of each single-phase VFD input (L1, L2) to smooth DC bus ripple and protect the 20 kW generator voltage regulator.
3. Trailer Chassis Bonding & Wet-Location Code (NEC 551 & 250)

Mobile water treatment trailers combine high-vibration highway transport with wet-process piping and chemical fumes:

  • Stranded Conductors Only: Solid building wire is prohibited on mobile trailers due to vibration fatigue. Use 100% Class B or Class C stranded copper with ferrules on all screw/spring terminals.
  • 316L SS Piping Equipotential Bond: Bond the 316L stainless process piping manifold, UF skid frame, and gooseneck chassis I-beams to the main ground busbar using #4 AWG braided tinned copper straps.
  • Exterior Grounding Lugs: Provide a heavy-duty bronze ground lug on the rear bumper (#4 AWG to 8-ft copper-clad ground rod when running on onboard generator).
