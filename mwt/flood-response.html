Calculation basis and limits

Standing water = area × depth ÷ 12 × 7.48052 US gallons/ft³. Average net removal = duty-point flow × effective-time fraction − continuing inflow. Pump time = initial gallons ÷ net GPM ÷ 60. Idealized drying days = moisture gallons ÷ (effective gallons/day/module × module count).

Pump flow must come from a selected pump curve at the actual total dynamic head, with suction conditions, hose losses and solids considered. The model does not calculate head, NPSH, evaporation, psychrometrics, electrical load or a material-specific dry standard. The two durations are not added: work may be staged or overlap only where site conditions allow. Measured moisture conditions govern completion.
