COORDINATED DEPLOYMENT

Three interfaces to resolve.

WATER

Intake & discharge

Define water condition, screen access, hose length and elevation to an authorized receiving point. The pumping trailer performs no treatment.

AIR

Supply & return

Reserve separate process-air return and dry-air supply connections. Keep regeneration wet-air exhaust and engine exhaust separate from building intakes.

SERVICE

Independent support

Each trailer retains local controls and service access. No shared control link or potable-water connection is required by this concept.
