316L SS TIG Welding, Elastomer Compatibility, Winterization Drains & Hydrostatic Test Log

Shop fabrication standards for highway trailer vibration, sanitary crevice elimination, and AWWA C651 disinfection.

1. 316L SS TIG Welding & Passivation (ASME B31.3 / AWS D18.1)
  • Material Grade: Dual-certified ASTM A312 TP316/316L (≤ 0.03% C) seamless or welded sanitary pipe (Sch 10S for flanged/grooved headers, 16 GA OD tube for sanitary tri-clamp).
  • 100% Argon Back-Purge: All butt welds on potable and UF permeate lines must be GTAW (TIG) welded with ER316L filler and 99.998% argon internal back-purging (< 50 ppm O₂) to prevent internal "sugaring" (granulation that harbors bacteria and shreds UF hollow fibers).
  • Citric Acid Passivation (ASTM A967): After fabrication, circulate 4%–10% food-grade citric acid at 120–140°F for 2 hours to remove free iron and form a passive chromium-oxide layer, then rinse to neutral pH.
2. Gasket & Seal Material Compatibility Matrix
  • Main Process & Potable Water (< 5 mg/L Free Cl₂): Use **NSF/ANSI 61 certified peroxide-cured EPDM** gaskets for all tri-clamps, butterfly valve seats, and ANSI 150# flanges. Never use standard plumbing red rubber or non-NSF neoprene!
  • Concentrated 12.5% Sodium Hypochlorite (P-502A/B Dosing): Concentrated bleach attacks EPDM over time. Use **Viton (FKM) O-rings and PTFE seats/diaphragms** with Sched 80 CPVC or PVDF injection quills.
  • Thread Sealant on NPT Instrumentation Ports: Use **NSF-61 Virgin PTFE tape** + potable-grade anaerobic stainless anti-galling paste (Loctite 567 / Nickel-PTFE) to prevent 316SS thread galling.
3. Highway Vibration Isolation & Freeze-Drain Slope
  • Pump Vibration Isolators (FC): Every pump suction and discharge connection (P-101, P-401, P-601, P-202) must include a double-sphere NSF-61 EPDM/PTFE expansion joint or braided 316L SS flex hose so road shocks on the trailer frame cannot crack pump volutes.
  • Cushioned Unistrut Pipe Clamps: Support all headers every 4.0 ft max using 316SS Unistrut channels with EPDM-cushioned Hydra-Zorb/ZSI clamps (prevents galvanic corrosion and road rattle).
  • 1/8" per Foot Pitch to Low-Point Drains (FD): Pitch all horizontal headers toward manual 1/2" 316SS quarter-turn drain valves so the entire plant drains in < 15 minutes for winter transport.
