Water Treatment Acronym Glossary

A plain-language reference for the system designs, simulators, drawings and business documents. Instrument numbers identify equipment; definitions do not establish treatment approval or certified performance.

Systems and treatment
BOD
Biochemical Oxygen Demand — oxygen consumed by biological breakdown during a specified test.
BWA
Boil Water Advisory — a notice affecting use of the water supply.
BW
Backwash — reverse-flow cleaning of a filter or membrane.
CIP
Clean-In-Place — cleaning installed process equipment without dismantling the treatment assembly.
COD
Chemical Oxygen Demand — oxygen-equivalent measure of chemically oxidizable material.
CT (disinfection)
Disinfectant concentration × contact time, usually mg·min/L; used to assess chemical disinfection under defined conditions.
DAF
Dissolved Air Flotation — fine bubbles lift separable material for removal.
DIT
Direct Integrity Test — a test for membrane defects, such as a pressure-based integrity check.
FC (water quality)
Free Chlorine — chlorine residual available in its free forms, as distinct from combined chlorine.
FDC
Fire Department Connection — a fire-protection connection, not the normal potable-water tie-in.
FOG / F.O.G.
Fats, Oils and Grease — materials encountered in restaurant and washwater waste streams.
GAC
Granular Activated Carbon — adsorbent media used for selected dissolved compounds, taste or odor duties.
MBR
Membrane Bioreactor — biological wastewater treatment combined with membrane separation; a different process from the proposed washwater train.
MWTP-50 / MWTP-50C / MWTP-250
AmeriPro model identifiers: restaurant self-contained, compact split-power, and hospital configurations respectively.
MWTP
Mobile Water Treatment Plant — the mobile treatment system family; model numbers are design labels, not guarantees of delivered flow.
pH
A measure of acidity or alkalinity; a logarithmic quantity rather than a conventional acronym.
PWR-25
Project name for the separate generator trailer; PWR denotes power.
RO
Reverse Osmosis — membrane treatment used to reduce dissolved salts; distinct from UF and not automatically included in these trains.
RPZ
Reduced Pressure Zone — a type of backflow-prevention assembly.
T10
Effective contact time based on the time when 10% of tracer has exited; accounts for short-circuiting through a contact system.
TDS
Total Dissolved Solids — dissolved material measured or estimated by a specified method.
TMP
Transmembrane Pressure — pressure difference driving flow through a membrane.
TSS
Total Suspended Solids — particles measured by the specified filtration-and-drying test.
UF
Ultrafiltration — membrane filtration of particles and microorganisms within the membrane’s validated capability; not universal dissolved-contaminant removal.
UVT
Ultraviolet Transmittance — how much UV light passes through a defined water sample; affects reactor performance.
UV
Ultraviolet — light used for disinfection in a validated reactor.
Measurements and materials
EPDM
Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer rubber — an elastomer used in seals; compatibility depends on service.
GPD
Gallons Per Day — daily flow or volume rate.
GPM
Gallons Per Minute — flow rate; these plans use US gallons.
HDPE
High-Density Polyethylene — a plastic used in tanks or piping where specified.
HP / BHP
Horsepower / Brake Horsepower — mechanical power; BHP refers to shaft power.
Hz
Hertz — cycles per second, used for electrical frequency.
ID / OD
Inside Diameter / Outside Diameter — pipe or component dimensions.
kVA
Kilovolt-ampere — apparent electrical power, distinct from real power in kW.
kW / kWh
Kilowatt measures power; kilowatt-hour measures energy. They are not interchangeable.
mg/L
Milligrams per Liter — concentration by mass per volume.
mJ/cm²
Millijoules per Square Centimeter — UV dose units.
NPSH
Net Positive Suction Head — suction-side head relevant to avoiding pump cavitation; available and required values differ.
NTU
Nephelometric Turbidity Unit — a light-scattering measure of water cloudiness, not proof of potability.
ppm
Parts Per Million — a concentration ratio; approximately mg/L for dilute water near 1 kg/L density.
PSI / psig
Pounds per Square Inch; psig specifies pressure relative to atmospheric pressure.
PVC
Polyvinyl Chloride — a plastic used in specified pipe and components.
PVDF
Polyvinylidene Fluoride — a polymer used in some membrane and process components.
SCH
Schedule — a pipe wall-thickness designation; not the same as pipe diameter.
SS / 304 SS / 316 SS
Stainless Steel; 304 and 316 identify alloy grades, not a water-quality certification.
TDH
Total Dynamic Head — total pumping head at the selected operating flow.
TIG
Tungsten Inert Gas welding — also called gas tungsten arc welding.
µm
Micrometer (micron) — one millionth of a meter; used for filter sizes.
Controls, electrical and drawing tags
A/B/C
Suffixes identifying parallel or repeated equipment, such as pumps; not separate treatment grades.
AC / DC
Alternating Current / Direct Current — two forms of electrical supply.
AIT
Analyzer Indicating Transmitter — measured water-quality signal; the drawing specifies which parameter.
ATS
Automatic Transfer Switch — transfers a load between electrical sources.
AWG
American Wire Gauge — wire-size designation.
BF / SM / BL
Project tag prefixes: bag filter / static mixer / blower.
CP
Control Panel — enclosure containing controls.
CT (electrical)
Current Transformer — an electrical-current sensor; different from disinfectant contact CT.
EMT
Electrical Metallic Tubing — metal electrical raceway.
FC / FO (valves)
Fail Closed / Fail Open — intended valve failure positions; FC here does not mean free chlorine.
FIT / FT
Flow Indicating Transmitter / Flow Transmitter — measures flow, with local indication where specified.
FS
Flow Switch — discrete indication of a flow condition.
GFCI
Ground-Fault Circuit Interrupter — protective device for specified ground-fault conditions.
HMI
Human-Machine Interface — operator screen and controls.
HVAC
Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning — environmental control equipment.
LFMC
Liquidtight Flexible Metal Conduit — flexible electrical raceway with a liquid-resistant covering.
LIT / LSH
Level Indicating Transmitter / Level Switch High — continuous level measurement / high-level switching.
MCP
Motor Circuit Protector — a protective device used in a coordinated motor circuit.
MDP
Main Distribution Panel — the panel distributing incoming electrical power.
N-G
Neutral-to-Ground — describes the neutral/ground relationship or bond in electrical drawings.
NO / NC
Normally Open / Normally Closed — contact or valve state; consult the drawing for the reference condition.
P / TK / STR
Project tag prefixes: pump / tank / strainer. A number identifies the specific item.
P&ID
Piping and Instrumentation Diagram — shows process connections, instruments and control relationships.
PE
Protective Earth on electrical drawings; Professional Engineer in engineering-service references.
PID (control)
Proportional–Integral–Derivative — a feedback-control method; filenames may also use PID as shorthand for P&ID.
PLC
Programmable Logic Controller — industrial computer that runs the process logic.
PSV
Pressure Safety Valve — pressure-protection valve, with selection and settings defined by the design.
PT / PI
Pressure Transmitter / Pressure Indicator — sends a pressure signal / displays pressure.
SOC / BMS / LFP
State of Charge / Battery Management System / Lithium Iron Phosphate — battery-related terms used in the hybrid-power companion concept.
TB
Terminal Block — field-wiring connection point on electrical drawings.
THWN-2
Thermoplastic Heat- and Water-resistant Nylon-coated wire; “-2” identifies a 90°C wet/dry insulation rating, not the allowable current of every installation.
UPS
Uninterruptible Power Supply — backup power for designated loads.
VFD
Variable Frequency Drive — controls a motor’s speed by adjusting electrical frequency.
XV
Project tag for an actuated on/off valve; its tag alone does not establish its fail position.
Standards, operations and business
ANSI
American National Standards Institute — coordinates the US voluntary standards system.
ASSE
American Society of Sanitary Engineering — name associated with plumbing performance standards and certification.
AWWA
American Water Works Association — water-industry organization and standards publisher.
BOM
Bill of Materials — equipment, parts and quantities list.
CapEx / OpEx
Capital Expenditure / Operating Expenditure — investment in assets / ongoing operating spending.
CDL
Commercial Driver’s License — driver credential; actual requirements depend on vehicle ratings and use.
COGS
Cost of Goods Sold — direct costs assigned to delivering the service or product.
DOT / CFR
Department of Transportation / Code of Federal Regulations — agency and federal regulatory compilation.
DSCR
Debt Service Coverage Ratio — available cash flow divided by debt payments, using the stated accounting basis.
EBITDA
Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization — earnings metric, not the same as cash available.
EPA
Environmental Protection Agency — US environmental agency.
EPD
Environmental Protection Division — Georgia’s environmental regulatory division in these documents.
GVWR / GCWR
Gross Vehicle Weight Rating / Gross Combination Weight Rating — rated limits for a vehicle / vehicle combination, not measured weights.
LOI
Letter of Intent — document expressing proposed commercial intent; obligations depend on its wording.
NEC
National Electrical Code — electrical installation code; applicable edition and jurisdiction matter.
NEMA
National Electrical Manufacturers Association — publishes electrical equipment and enclosure standards.
NSF / NSF-61
NSF is the certification/standards organization; NSF/ANSI/CAN 61 concerns drinking-water system component health effects, not certification of the entire treatment process.
OEM
Original Equipment Manufacturer — supplier responsible for a component or package.
PPE
Personal Protective Equipment — task-specific protective clothing and equipment.
QA / QC
Quality Assurance / Quality Control — planned quality processes / checks of results.
ROI
Return on Investment — financial return relative to investment under stated assumptions.
SBA
Small Business Administration — US agency associated with the loan program discussed.
SDS
Safety Data Sheet — chemical hazard and handling information.
SOP
Standard Operating Procedure — documented operating steps.
SRW / DRW
Single Rear Wheel / Dual Rear Wheel — truck wheel configurations.
TBD / REV / DWG
To Be Determined / Revision / Drawing — document and design-status shorthand.
UCC-1
Uniform Commercial Code financing statement — filing used to give notice of a security interest.
UL / UL 508A
UL standards/certification designation; UL 508A addresses industrial control panels.
USD
United States Dollars — currency used in the estimates.
