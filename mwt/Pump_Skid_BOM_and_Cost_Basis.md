The base allowance assumes standard industrial wetted materials (ductile iron / 304 SS manifolds) and NEMA 4 enclosure construction. Upgrading all wetted pumps, manifolds and valves to 316L stainless steel or duplex alloy for corrosive brines or hot chemical washwater, or specifying explosion-proof (Class I Div 1/2) motors and controls, will materially increase capital cost.

## Exclusions and unresolved costs

Sales taxes, freight, crane/rigging mobilization, concrete housekeeping pad, anchor bolts, external suction/discharge hoses or site piping, electrical feeder cable, upstream transformer/generator (`HybridForce-60` or building switchgear), spare mechanical seal kits, freeze protection / heat tracing and third-party structural/UL panel certification fees beyond the base shop allowance are excluded.

## Supplier quote requirements

Request published pump curves showing flow, head, efficiency, NPSHr, minimum continuous stable flow and brake horsepower to runout; wetted material certificates; mechanical seal flush plan; motor efficiency and inverter turndown torque rating; manifold hydrotest pressure; panel short-circuit current rating (SCCR) and enclosure thermal dissipation at maximum ambient temperature.

## Commercial deployment notes

Because `PPS-200` is a modular drop-in asset, evaluate whether a single standardized stainless-manifold specification can serve both stationary plant duties (`GREX-1000` screened aqueous transfer) and mobile rental / `Reclaim-25` tank-farm deployments, reducing spare-parts inventory across the AmeriPro fleet.
