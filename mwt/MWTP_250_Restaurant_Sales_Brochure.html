Why Boiling Pots of Water Can't Run a Commercial Kitchen

Kitchens need pressurized, safe water at every fixture. Sediment stirred up by main breaks can also foul valves, solenoids, and filters in kitchen equipment.

1. Dish Machines & 3-Comp Sinks

Steady 60–70 PSI so dish machines reach their rated rinse pressure.

2. Ice Machines, Soda & Espresso

0.02-micron ultrafiltration removes sediment and microbes before water reaches your equipment. Follow your health department's instructions on ice and beverage lines after any advisory.

3. Customer & Employee Restrooms

Restrooms and hand sinks need water pressure to stay open. We pressurize the whole building's domestic system.

4. No Semi-Truck Needed

Our 36-ft gooseneck is towed by a heavy-duty truck driven by a CDL-licensed operator, and fits lots where 53-ft semis can't. Plan for the trailer plus two ground tanks (about 3–4 parking spaces).

HOW THE KITCHEN TIE-IN WORKS

How We Connect to Your Restaurant

We map your connection in advance with you, your plumber, and the water utility. On the day:

Step 1 — Safe Water Supply: With the utility's permit, hydrant water runs through the utility's meter and backflow device and a valve that keeps the city main at 20 PSI or more, into our air-gap break tank. If the main has no pressure, we bring water in by tanker.
Step 2 — Treatment & Our Own Power: Our onboard 80 kW diesel generator powers 0.02-micron ultrafiltration (integrity-tested daily), validated UV, and chlorination with a measured contact time.
Step 3 — Isolate & Connect: The utility closes your curb stop (so treated water doesn't flow back into the street), and we connect a sanitized 1.5" or 2" NSF-61 hose to the connection point your plumber prepared on the building side of your meter or backflow assembly.
Step 4 — Records for Your Inspector: Our state-certified operator logs turbidity, UV dose, and chlorine readings, and sends bacteriological samples to a certified lab, giving your health inspector what they need to evaluate the alternate source.
