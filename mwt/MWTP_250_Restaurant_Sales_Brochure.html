Why Boiling Pots of Water Can't Run a Commercial Kitchen

Kitchens need pressurized, safe water at every fixture. Sediment stirred up by main breaks can also foul valves, solenoids, and filters in kitchen equipment.

1. Dish Machines & 3-Comp Sinks

Steady 60–75 PSI and up to 50 GPM peak flow from our onboard 150-gal clearwell and duplex VFD booster so dish machines reach rated rinse pressure.

2. Ice Machines, Soda & Espresso

0.02-micron ultrafiltration, carbon block polishing, UV, and chlorine remove sediment and microbes before water reaches your equipment. Follow your health department's instructions on ice and beverage lines after any advisory.

3. Customer & Employee Restrooms

Restrooms and hand sinks need water pressure to stay open. We pressurize the whole building's domestic system.

HOW THE KITCHEN TIE-IN WORKS

How We Connect to Your Restaurant

We map your connection in advance alongside our regular F.O.G. (grease trap) and hood service visits, coordinating with you, your plumber, and the water utility. On the day:

Step 1 — Safe Water Supply (3 Options): During a Boil Water Advisory where the street main still has pressure, we draw from your upstream exterior tap (before your closed building shutoff) or a permitted hydrant through a valve that keeps city pressure at 20 PSI or more, into our 550-gal air-gap break tank. If the main has zero pressure, a single 4,000-gal potable tanker drop supplies a typical kitchen for a full day.
Step 2 — Multi-Barrier Treatment & Dual Power: Powered silently by your 240V 50A shore receptacle (or our onboard 20 kW diesel generator on the gooseneck deck), the unit runs 0.02-micron ultrafiltration (integrity-tested daily), validated UV, and chlorination through a 600-gal baffled contact tank.
Step 3 — Isolate & Connect: The utility (or your plumber with utility permission) closes your street shutoff so treated water doesn't flow back into the street, and we connect a sanitized 1.5" or 2" NSF-61 hose to the domestic connection point on the building side of your meter/backflow assembly. Backwash water goes to your downstream sanitary cleanout — never through your grease trap.
Step 4 — Records for Your Inspector: Our state-certified operator logs daily membrane integrity tests, turbidity, UV dose, and chlorine CT readings, and sends bacteriological samples to a certified lab, giving your health inspector what they need to evaluate the alternate source.
